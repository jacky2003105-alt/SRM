第 1 題

A life insurance company is underwriting a potential insured as Preferred or Standard, for the purpose of determining the premium. Insureds with lower expected mortality rates are Preferred. The company will use factors such as credit rating, occupation, and blood pressure. The company constructs a decision tree, based on its past experience, to determine whether the potential insured is Preferred or Standard.

Determine, from a statistical learning perspective, which of the following describes this underwriting method.

I. Classification setting

II. Parametric

III. Supervised

(A) None

(B) I and II only

(C) I and III only

(D) II and III only

(E) The correct answer is not given by (A), (B), (C), or (D).

看中文題意（不影響作答）

壽險公司用信用評等、職業、血壓等因素，依過去經驗建立決策樹，判斷新保戶屬於優體或標準體。從統計學習的角度，下列哪些描述這個核保方法？I. 分類問題。II. 參數方法。III. 監督式。

答案

(C)

解析

I 對：預測的是類別（優體／標準體）。III 對：用過去「已知類別」的資料訓練，有反應變數。II 錯：決策樹不假設 \(f\) 的函數形式，是非參數方法（第 1 章、第 16 章）。

第 2 題

An insurance company is modeling the probability of a claim using logistic regression. The explanatory variable is vehicle value. Vehicle value is banded, and the value of the variable is 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6, depending on the band. Band 1 is the reference level.

The fitted value of the \(\beta\) corresponding to band 4 is \(-0.695\).

Let \(O_1\) be the odds of a claim for a policy in band 1, and \(O_4\) the odds of a claim for a policy in band 4.

Determine \(O_4/O_1\).

(A) 0.30

(B) 0.35

(C) 0.40

(D) 0.45

(E) 0.50

看中文題意（不影響作答）

logistic 迴歸預測理賠機率，車價分 6 個級距（類別變數），第 1 級為基準。第 4 級的係數 \(-0.695\)。求第 4 級與第 1 級的理賠勝算比 \(O_4/O_1\)。

答案

(E)

解析

logit 連結下，線性預測式就是對數勝算。第 4 級與基準級的對數勝算相差 \(\beta_4\)，所以勝算比 \(=e^{-0.695}=0.4991\)（第 12 章 12.1 節）。

易錯

這裡的級距是類別變數的指標，不是數值 4 乘上一個斜率；直接用 \(e^{\beta_4}\)。

第 3 題

Auto liability claim size is modeled using a generalized linear model. Based on an analysis of the data, it is believed that the coefficient of variation of claim size is constant.

Which of the following response distributions would be most appropriate to use?

(A) Poisson

(B) Normal

(C) Gamma

(D) Inverse Gamma

(E) Inverse Gaussian

看中文題意（不影響作答）

汽車責任險理賠金額用 GLM 建模，資料顯示理賠金額的變異係數固定。哪一個反應分配最適合？

答案

(C)

解析

變異係數 \(=\sigma/\mu\) 固定，等價於 \(\mathrm{Var}(Y)=c\,\mu^2\)，變異數與平均的平方成正比。這正是 gamma 的變異數函數 \(V(\mu)=\mu^2\)（第 11 章的變異數函數表）。Poisson 是 \(\mu\)、常態是常數、逆高斯是 \(\mu^3\)。

第 4 題

You are given the following output from a GLM to estimate loss size:

(i) Distribution selected is Inverse Gaussian.

(ii) The link is \(g(\mu)=1/\mu^2\).

Parameter \(\beta\)
Intercept 0.00279
Vehicle Body: Coupe 0.002
Vehicle Body: Sedan −0.001
Vehicle Body: SUV 0.003
Vehicle Value (000) −0.00007
Area: B −0.025
Area: C 0.015
Area: D 0.005

Calculate mean loss size for a sedan with value 25,000 from Area A.

(A) 80

(B) 160

(C) 320

(D) 640

(E) 1280

看中文題意（不影響作答）

逆高斯 GLM，連結 \(g(\mu)=1/\mu^2\)，係數如表。求價值 25,000 的轎車、A 區的平均損失。

答案

(B)

解析

A 區是基準（表中沒有 A 的係數），車價單位是千元，所以 \(x=25\)： \[\eta=0.00279-0.001+25(-0.00007)=0.00004 .\] 連結 \(1/\mu^2=\eta\)，所以 \(\mu=1/\sqrt{0.00004}=158.11\)，最接近 160。

易錯

注意連結的反函數：\(\mu=\eta^{-1/2}\)，不是 \(1/\eta\)（那會得到 25,000）。

第 5 題

For a generalized linear model,

(i) There are 72 observations.

(ii) There are 25 parameters.

(iii) The loglikelihood is −361.24

You are considering adding a cubic polynomial variable.

Determine the lowest loglikelihood for which this additional variable would not be rejected at 1% significance.

(A) −358

(B) −356

(C) −354

(D) −352

(E) −350

看中文題意（不影響作答）

GLM 有 72 筆觀察、25 個參數，對數概似 −361.24。考慮加入一個三次多項式變數。要讓這個新變數在 1% 下不被拒絕，對數概似最低要到多少？

答案

(B)

解析

三次多項式（\(x\)、\(x^2\)、\(x^3\)）增加 3 個參數。概似比檢定 \(2(\ell_{\text{新}}-\ell_{\text{舊}})\sim\chi^2_3\)，1% 臨界值 11.345： \[\ell_{\text{新}}\ge-361.24+\frac{11.345}{2}=-355.57 .\] 選項中最接近的是 −356（講義的答案）。

易錯

自由度是新增參數的個數 3，不是 1；觀察數 72 用不到。

第 6 題

In the following decision tree, the RSS of the observations at each node are displayed. For example, at the lowest left node, \(\sum(y_i-\bar y)^2=10\) for the observations \(y_i\) that are at that node.

The tree is pruned using cost complexity pruning.

Determine the lowest \(\alpha\) for which two branches are pruned.

(A) 6

(B) 7

(C) 10

(D) 12

(E) 13

看中文題意（不影響作答）

決策樹每個節點標出該節點觀察的 RSS。用成本複雜度修剪，求剛好剪掉兩個分支的最小 \(\alpha\)。

答案

(B)

解析

剪掉一個分支（把兩片葉子併回父節點）讓 RSS 增加，葉子少 1。值得剪的門檻是「RSS 增加量 ÷ 減少的葉子數」（第 16 章 16.3 節）： - 左分支：\(24-(10+8)=6\)。 - 右分支：\(22-(8+7)=7\)。

\(\alpha\ge6\) 時剪掉左分支；\(\alpha\ge7\) 時右分支也被剪掉。兩個都剪需要 \(\alpha=7\)。（再剪根節點要 \(56-(24+22)=10\)，那是第三次修剪。）

第 8 題

A generalized linear model for automobile insurance with 40 observations has the following explanatory variables:

  • SEX (male or female)
  • AGE (4 levels)
  • TYPE OF VEHICLE (sedan, coupe, SUV, van)
  • MILES DRIVEN (continuous variable)
  • USE (business, pleasure, farm)

Model I includes all of these variables and an intercept. Model II is the same as Model I except that it excludes USE. You have the following statistics from these models:

Deviance AIC
Model I 23.12 58.81
Model II 62.61

Using the likelihood ratio test, which of the following statements is correct?

(A) Reject Model II at 0.5% significance.

(B) Reject Model II at 1.0% significance but not at 0.5% significance.

(C) Reject Model II at 2.5% significance but not at 1.0% significance.

(D) Reject Model II at 5.0% significance but not at 2.5% significance.

(E) Do not reject Model II at 5.0% significance.

看中文題意（不影響作答）

40 筆觀察的汽車險 GLM。模型 I 含全部變數，模型 II 拿掉 USE（3 類）。模型 I 的離差 23.12、AIC 58.81；模型 II 的 AIC 62.61。用概似比檢定判斷。

答案

(C)

解析

USE 有 3 類，用 2 個指標，所以模型 II 少 2 個參數。AIC \(=-2\ell+2p\)： \[\text{AIC}_{II}-\text{AIC}_{I}=\big(-2\ell_{II}+2\ell_I\big)-2(2)=3.80\ \Rightarrow\ 2(\ell_I-\ell_{II})=3.80+4=7.80 .\] LRT \(=7.80\)，自由度 2。\(\chi^2_2\) 的臨界值：2.5% 是 7.378、1% 是 9.210。在 2.5% 拒絕、在 1% 不拒絕。

易錯

AIC 的差不是 LRT：要把懲罰項的差 \(2\times2=4\) 加回去。模型 II 的離差沒給，也不需要。

第 9 題

In a classification decision tree, there is one predictor, \(X\). In one region, there are five observations with the following values:

\(X\) 3 5 7 9 11
\(Y\) 0 0 1 0 1

This region is split using classification error as the criterion.

The classification error of a split is the sum of the probabilities of the subregions times the classification errors of the subregions.

Determine the classification error of the optimal split.

(A) 0.1

(B) 0.2

(C) 0.3

(D) 0.4

(E) 0.5

看中文題意（不影響作答）

一個區域有 5 筆觀察，用分類錯誤率作為分割準則（各子區域的錯誤率依比例加權）。求最佳分割的分類錯誤率。

答案

(B)

解析

子區域的錯誤率是少數類別的比例。四種分割： - \(X<4\)：\(\{0\}\) 與 \(\{0,1,0,1\}\)：\(\frac45\cdot\frac12=0.4\)。 - \(X<6\)：\(\{0,0\}\) 與 \(\{1,0,1\}\)：\(\frac35\cdot\frac13=0.2\)。 - \(X<8\)：\(\{0,0,1\}\) 與 \(\{0,1\}\)：\(\frac35\cdot\frac13+\frac25\cdot\frac12=0.4\)。 - \(X<10\)：\(\{0,0,1,0\}\) 與 \(\{1\}\)：\(\frac45\cdot\frac14=0.2\)。

最小 0.2（兩種分割同分）。

第 10 題

A normal linear model with 2 variables and an intercept is based on 45 observations. \(\hat y_j\) is the fitted value of \(y_j\), and \(\hat y_{j(i)}\) is the fitted value of \(y_j\) if observation \(i\) is removed. You are given:

(i) \(\sum_{j=1}^{45}(\hat y_j-\hat y_{j(1)})^2=4.1\).

(ii) The leverage of the first observation is 0.15.

Determine \(|\hat\varepsilon_1|\), the absolute value of the first residual of the regression with no observation removed.

(A) 3.9

(B) 4.4

(C) 4.9

(D) 5.4

(E) 5.9

看中文題意（不影響作答）

45 筆觀察、2 個變數加截距。刪掉第 1 筆後所有配適值的變化平方和 4.1，第 1 筆的槓桿 0.15。求第 1 筆殘差的絕對值。

答案

(B)

解析

Cook 距離的兩種寫法（第 5 章 5.2 節）： \[D_1=\frac{\sum_j(\hat y_j-\hat y_{j(1)})^2}{(k+1)s^2}=\frac{e_1^2}{(k+1)s^2}\cdot\frac{h_{11}}{(1-h_{11})^2}.\] 兩邊的 \((k+1)s^2\) 相消： \[4.1=e_1^2\cdot\frac{0.15}{0.85^2}\ \Rightarrow\ e_1^2=\frac{4.1(0.85)^2}{0.15}=19.748,\qquad|e_1|=4.444 .\]

易錯

用原始殘差時分母是 \((1-h)^2\)；不需要知道 \(s^2\)。

第 5 章 線性迴歸：模型診斷

第 11 題

A least squares model with a large number of predictors is fitted to 90 observations. To reduce the number of predictors, forward stepwise selection is performed.

For a model with \(k\) predictors, \(\text{RSS}=c_k\).

The estimated variance of the error of the fit is \(\hat\sigma^2=40\).

Determine the value of \(c_d-c_{d+1}\) for which you would be indifferent between the \(d+1\)-predictor model and the \(d\)-predictor model based on Mallow's \(C_p\).

(A) 40

(B) 50

(C) 60

(D) 70

(E) 80

看中文題意（不影響作答）

90 筆觀察做向前逐步選擇，\(k\) 個變數的 RSS 是 \(c_k\)，\(\hat\sigma^2=40\)。RSS 減少多少時，依 \(C_p\) 對 \(d\) 與 \(d+1\) 個變數的模型無差異？

答案

(E)

解析

\(C_p=\frac1n(\text{RSS}+2d\hat\sigma^2)\)。令兩個模型相等： \[c_d+2d(40)=c_{d+1}+2(d+1)(40)\ \Rightarrow\ c_d-c_{d+1}=2(40)=80 .\] 多一個變數的懲罰是 \(2\hat\sigma^2\)（第 7 章 7.2 節的門檻推導）。Frees 的 \(C_p\) 公式結論相同。
